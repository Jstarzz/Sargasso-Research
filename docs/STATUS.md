# Research status — 27 October 2026

The newest dated research record in the sources is 16 October 2026.

## IN PROGRESS: prospective daily shadow evaluation of frozen Model 2 candidate C

A signed protocol fixes an issue window from 25 September 2026 through 14 January 2027. Candidate C and a persistence baseline are issued daily and scored against later observations. **No interim skill numbers are published; none will be until the window closes.** If fewer than 30 cases or 4 time blocks are scored, the protocol records INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE rather than extending the window.

## Sub-kilometre coastal forecasting feasibility (Putman GPS tracks)

Four frozen-protocol retrospective hindcast phases ran on 19 to 20 September 2026: [currents only](../experiments/subkm-putman-currents-only-2026-09-20/README.md), [windage sweep](../experiments/subkm-putman-windage-sweep-2026-09-20/README.md), [windage validation](../experiments/subkm-putman-windage-validation-2026-09-20/README.md) and [Stokes drift](../experiments/subkm-putman-stokes-2026-09-20/README.md). They support at most a modest windage effect on one 2018 deployment near Puerto Rico. **No 500 m or sub-kilometre skill, and no St Kitts validation, is claimed.** The earlier "in progress" status of 19 September is superseded.

## Evidence boundaries since the last release

- The once-only June-September 2026 final check failed on 29 September and its cohort was consumed. The later C3 versus C and P comparison on that cohort is a disclosed second look and did not pass against C. See the [timeline entry](../TIMELINE.md).
- R49 beat the selected Model 1 ensemble on the SKN cohort but lost on the historical official test. See both R49 records in the [experiment index](../EXPERIMENTS.md).
- A clean re-evaluation on 6 October found the R18 learned correction matched a constant-shift control on 19 held-out pairs. This challenges earlier R18 results but is small and unresolved; see the [R18 record](../experiments/m2-r18-clean-reevaluation-2026-10-06/README.md).
- Model 2 gains reported for drifters (observation-based current product, track-history correction) confirmed on real GPS-tracked mats off Barbados and Jamaica but not on the 2018 Puerto Rico mats or Sargassum-like suites. See the [Barbados and Jamaica record](../experiments/m2-real-mat-gps-barbados-jamaica-2026-10/README.md) and the [Sargassum suites record](../experiments/m2-track-history-sargassum-suites-2026-10-08/README.md).
- Model 1 training is on hold and guarded validation remains blocked as of 14 October. Model 3 skill is unmeasurable without verified beach observations.
- No model has been promoted and no serving scope changed.

## Scope of this archive

This release is a research record, not a claim that a service is deployed. Seven-day outlooks, six-hour output intervals, sub-kilometre coastal skill, and live beach-arrival performance are not established by the completed results released here.

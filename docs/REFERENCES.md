## Methods and scientific context

1. Ronneberger, O., Fischer, P., & Brox, T. (2015). **U-Net: Convolutional Networks for Biomedical Image Segmentation.** [arXiv:1505.04597](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.04597). Architectural foundation; not a citation for Sargasso performance.
2. Tan, M., & Le, Q. V. (2019). **EfficientNet: Rethinking Model Scaling for Convolutional Neural Networks.** [arXiv:1905.11946](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.11946). Encoder-family context; not Sargasso evaluation evidence.
3. Wang, M., & Hu, C. (2021). **Satellite remote sensing of pelagic Sargassum macroalgae: The power of high resolution and deep learning.** *Remote Sensing of Environment*, 264, 112631. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2021.112631); [NOAA bibliographic record](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/56090). Relevant remote-sensing literature, not an independent validation of this project.
4. Hu, C. (2009). **A novel ocean color index to detect floating algae in the global oceans.** *Remote Sensing of Environment*, 113(10), 2118–2129. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2009.05.012). Floating Algae Index foundation; does not make automatically generated labels independent ground truth.
5. Putman, N. F., Lumpkin, R., Olascoaga, M. J., Trinanes, J., & Goni, G. J. (2020). **Improving transport predictions of pelagic Sargassum.** *Journal of Experimental Marine Biology and Ecology*, 529, 151398. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jembe.2020.151398); [NOAA bibliographic record](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/39499). Transport-study context; citation does not establish the identity or redistribution rights of the local GPS case files.
6. Lellouche, J.-M., et al. (2021). **The Copernicus Global 1/12° Oceanic and Sea Ice GLORYS12 Reanalysis.** *Frontiers in Earth Science*, 9, 698876. [DOI and publisher](https://doi.org/10.3389/feart.2021.698876). Reanalysis-system context; the exact product and release used by a completed experiment require their own provenance.
7. Hersbach, H., et al. (2020). **The ERA5 global reanalysis.** *Quarterly Journal of the Royal Meteorological Society*, 146, 1999–2049. [DOI and publisher](https://doi.org/10.1002/qj.3803). Atmospheric-reanalysis context; retrospective ERA5 input is not issue-time forecast forcing.

# Model 3 island and coastal stretch recovery results

## Question

What evidence did the disclosed recovery provide for island risk and coastal stretch risk?

## Method

We summarize aggregate island outcome counts and coastal stretch AUC comparisons from the previously exposed June-September 2026 cohort. The primary stretch comparison is at 7 days; the 2-day comparison is descriptive.

## Result

Island v3 had 154, 155, 151, and 149 positives at 2, 3, 5, and 7 days, respectively, with zero negatives at every horizon. AUC evidence is insufficient.

The primary coastal stretch 7-day comparison on n=419 had v3 AUC 0.59556 versus climatology K 0.50798. The delta was +0.08759, with a 95% interval of [-0.02779, 0.14468], which is inconclusive. The descriptive 2-day comparison on n=431 had delta +0.11593 with interval [0.02681, 0.17622].

## Interpretation

The island results do not support AUC assessment because there are no negative outcomes at the reported horizons. The primary coastal stretch result is inconclusive. The 2-day result remains descriptive.

## Limitations

This is a disclosed second look on a previously exposed cohort, not independent confirmation or a fresh holdout. Under the signed label definition, an island positive is any occupied zone cell even when eligibility is false; a negative requires at least 80% eligible coverage. The label semantics and seasonal prevalence limit interpretation as natural prevalence.
